## Pricing types

Use [Quote purchase](/api-reference/endpoints/purchases/quote) to get the order total.

### Standard pricing (`pricing_type: "standard"`)

For selectable quantities, `discounts` maps each range's first unit to a discount percentage. Each range ends just before the next threshold. Earlier units keep their price.

With a unit price of **1** and `discounts: {"1": 0, "100": 10}`, 150 units for one hour or tariff period cost:

- Units 1–99: `99 × 1 = 99`
- Units 100–150: `51 × 1 × 0.90 = 45.90`
- Total before user discounts: **144.90**

For hourly tariffs, multiply by the number of hours. The server then applies the effective user discount and rounds to two decimal places.

For selectable quantities, the API's `price` already includes discounts for `count.min` and one hour or tariff period. The base unit price is not returned.

### Graduated pricing (`pricing_type: "graduated"`)

`graduated_prices` maps quantity thresholds to prices with discounts included. Use the highest threshold that does not exceed the quantity.

For `{"0": 135, "101": 180, "201": 270}`:

- 50 units cost **135**
- 150 units cost **180**
- 250 units cost **270**

For hourly tariffs, multiply by the number of hours for an estimate. Tier prices are already rounded; use [Quote purchase](/api-reference/endpoints/purchases/quote) for the final total.

The `discounts` field does not apply to graduated pricing.
