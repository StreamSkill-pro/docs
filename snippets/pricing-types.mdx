## Pricing

Use [POST /purchases/quote](/api-reference/endpoints/purchases/quote) to get the final order total. To calculate it yourself, use the tariff's `pricing` object and the rules below.

Prices are in `pricing.currency`, before discounts, for one hour (`hourly: true`) or one tariff period (`hourly: false`).

| Type | Subtotal calculation |
| --- | --- |
| `standard` | Unit prices with separate quantity discounts for each range |
| `fixed` | Price of a fixed-quantity package |
| `graduated` | One price for the entire selected quantity, determined by its tier |

`discount` is the effective percentage for your account and this tariff, including promotions. Use the returned value as-is. Quantity discounts are separate. Do not apply `/me.discount` again.

For all three types, calculate the subtotal as described below, then:

```text
total = round(subtotal × duration × (1 - discount / 100), 2)
```

**Use `duration = 1` when `hourly: false`.** In the request, omit `duration` or send `1`. For hourly tariffs, use the requested number of hours; omitting `duration` also defaults to `1`.

Round only the final total. Paid orders have a minimum total of `0.01`.

### Standard

`price_per_unit` is the base price of one unit. `quantity_discounts` maps each range's first unit to a discount percentage. A range ends just before the next threshold; earlier units keep their discount. The returned schedule reflects your account's terms. An empty object `{}` means no quantity discount.

```json
{
  "type": "standard",
  "currency": "usd",
  "price_per_unit": 1,
  "quantity_discounts": {"1": 0, "100": 10},
  "discount": 10
}
```

```text
subtotal = sum(units_in_range × price_per_unit × (1 - range_discount / 100))
```

The first 99 units cost **1 USD each**; from the 100th unit onward, each costs **0.90 USD**. The discount does not reprice earlier units.

For 150 units, the subtotal is: `99 × 1 + 51 × 1 × 0.90 = 144.90 USD`. With no quantity discounts, the subtotal is simply `quantity × price_per_unit`.

### Fixed

`price` covers the whole package. The tariff's `count` is fixed, so do not multiply by quantity. Quantity discounts do not apply.

```json
{
  "type": "fixed",
  "currency": "usd",
  "price": 100,
  "discount": 10
}
```

```text
subtotal = price
```

With `duration = 1`, the total is **90 USD**. For an hourly package, multiply by the requested hours before applying the discount.

### Graduated

`graduated_prices` maps quantity thresholds to prices for the entire selected quantity. Choose the highest threshold that does not exceed `quantity`. If the quantity is below every threshold, use the first tier. Do not add tier prices or multiply by quantity. Quantity discounts do not apply.

```json
{
  "type": "graduated",
  "currency": "usd",
  "graduated_prices": {"0": 100, "101": 150, "201": 200},
  "discount": 10
}
```

```text
subtotal = selected_tier_price
```

For 150 units, the subtotal is `150`. With `duration = 2` and `discount = 10`, the total is **270 USD**.

### Legacy fields

Top-level `price`, `pricing_type`, `graduated_prices` and `discounts` remain for compatibility. Use `pricing` for new integrations.

Unlike the prices inside `pricing`, top-level `price` and `graduated_prices` already include discounts and rounding. `price` covers the default quantity with `duration = 1`. The old `pricing_type` reports `standard` for both standard and fixed tariffs.
